## Design Context

### Users
Greek local-business owners and marketers evaluating flyer distribution from a phone, tablet, or desktop. They need to understand service coverage, delivery methods, documentation, and how quickly they can request a quote.

### Brand Personality
Local, accountable, and practical. The interface should create confidence through concrete Athens context and clear operational proof rather than exaggerated marketing claims.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the existing Habitline-derived light editorial template, strong photography, rounded compositions, and restrained colored accents. Improve its responsiveness and local authenticity without redesigning the site or replacing the hero image.

### Design Principles
- Keep Athens recognizable while distinguishing illustrative or stock imagery from completed-campaign evidence.
- Make every composition stable from 320px through 1920px without clipping, overlap, or hidden content.
- Preserve the template hierarchy; use scoped adaptation CSS instead of modifying compiled assets.
- Prioritize readable service explanations and quote actions over decorative motion.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and touch-sized controls.
