## Download Workflow

1. Open Pexels or Unsplash
2. Copy-paste search term
3. Find high-quality image (960x640px+, professional, relevant)
4. Download "Large" or "Original" size
5. Save with temporary name to `~/Downloads/greenpromo-images/`
6. Repeat for all 26 images
7. Rename per `IMAGE_REPLACEMENT_GUIDE.md` convention
8. Optimize with script
9. Move to `greenpromo/images/`

---

## Quality Checklist

For each image, verify:
- [ ] Sharp, clear, high resolution
- [ ] Professional appearance
- [ ] Relevant to business/service
- [ ] Good lighting
- [ ] Natural colors
- [ ] No heavy watermarks
- [ ] Aspect ratio ~3:2 (or croppable)
- [ ] No distracting elements
