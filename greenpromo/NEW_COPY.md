## SECTION 9: FAQ SAMPLES (High-Value Questions)

### Q: What's the minimum order quantity?
**A:** It depends on the product. Most merchandise starts at 50–100 units, but we offer low-minimum options for custom items and small businesses. Discuss your needs with our team for flexible solutions.

### Q: Can you rush production?
**A:** Absolutely. We offer express 3–5 day turnaround for most items, though this may impact pricing. Contact us with your timeline, and we'll confirm feasibility.

### Q: Do you offer eco-friendly options?
**A:** Yes. We source sustainable materials including organic cotton apparel, recycled drinkware, and compostable packaging. Many clients prioritize green options to align with their brand values.

### Q: Can you help with design?
**A:** Yes. Our design team assists with logo placement, color matching, and layout. We provide digital mockups for approval before production. Design consultation is included with all tiered packages.

### Q: How long does production typically take?
**A:** Standard orders ship in 7–14 business days. Rush options available (3–5 days). Timeline depends on product type, customization complexity, and order volume.

### Q: What's your quality guarantee?
**A:** Every order passes strict quality inspection before shipping. We verify logo accuracy, color matching, and material quality. If items don't meet our standards, we redo them at no charge.

### Q: Do you handle packaging?
**A:** Yes. We offer standard, premium, and custom packaging options. From simple branded boxes to luxury gift sets with tissue, stickers, and custom messaging—we create memorable unboxing experiences.

### Q: Can I order samples first?
**A:** Highly recommended. We provide samples (usually at discounted rates) so you can evaluate quality, color accuracy, and fit before committing to full production.

### Q: What payment methods do you accept?
**A:** Bank transfer, credit card, and invoice payment available. For larger orders, we offer flexible payment terms and deposit-balance options.

### Q: Do you handle international orders?
**A:** Yes. We ship worldwide. Costs vary by location. Contact us with your delivery destination for shipping quotes.

## SECTION 12: BRAND MESSAGING GUIDELINES

### Tone of Voice
- **Professional yet approachable:** We're experts, but we make the process easy and friendly.
- **Trustworthy & reliable:** Clients depend on us for important brand moments.
- **Eco-conscious & modern:** Sustainability isn't a feature—it's part of who we are.
- **Results-focused:** We highlight outcomes (engagement, loyalty, brand impact) not just products.

### Key Messaging Principles
1. **End-to-End:** Emphasize that we handle everything from concept to delivery.
2. **Quality-First:** Highlight rigorous quality assurance and attention to detail.
3. **Sustainability:** Promote eco-friendly options as standard, not premium.
4. **Customization:** Show the breadth of options and full personalization capabilities.
5. **Relationship-Focused:** Position products as brand ambassadors that strengthen relationships.

### Avoid
- Generic "promotional products" language
- Overly casual tone
- Jargon without explanation
- Empty superlatives ("best," "amazing")
- Focus on price over value
