## Comparative Summary

| Dimension | Digiastar | Synergic |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| Founded | 2021 | 2005 |
| Focus | Revenue/marketing-driven digital agency | Technical eCommerce automation |
| Strength | ROAS results, awards, active content | Technical depth, ISO cert, enterprise integrations |
| Weakness | Tiny team for scope of claims | Outdated content, no portfolio, no social proof |
| Content | Active blog + webinars | Dead blog (last post 2022) |
| Innovation | AI phone agent, AutoPricing partner platform | Broad but dated tech stack |
| Transparency | Named CEO, testimonials, metrics | Anonymous team, no case studies |
| Market | Greek SMEs wanting growth | Greek SMEs wanting e-shops |

Digiastar is younger but much more aggressively marketed with tangible results and active thought leadership. Synergic has the tenure and technical breadth but appears to have stagnated in terms of public presence and content. Both serve the Greek SME eCommerce market from Athens with small teams.
