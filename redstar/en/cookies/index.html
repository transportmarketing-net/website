1. What are cookies and local storage

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by the browser when you visit a website. localStorage is similar technology that allows storing data locally in the browser. They are used for functional, statistical, or advertising purposes.

2. What storage the website uses

The website transportmarketing.net/redstar does not use advertising cookies. We use:

  • Essential storage: Only for cookie choice (localStorage).
  • Optional statistics: Google Analytics, only after consent.

3. Essential storage

This storage is essential for the cookie choice system to function and cannot be disabled without affecting functionality.

redstar-consent-v1localStorageStores your choice (Accept or Essential only)Permanent (until deletion)

Does not contain: Personal information, IP address, or tracking identifiers.

4. Optional statistics cookies (Google Analytics)

If you choose Accept in the cookie banner, Google Analytics (G-212798DHFW) loads. These cookies collect anonymous or pseudonymized data to understand which pages users visit.

Important: These cookies do not load if you do not choose Accept. If you choose "Essential only", no tracking occurs.

5. Third-party services

5.1 Google Fonts

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google may log the request (IP, time) for operational purposes. It does not place tracking cookies.

5.2 Google Calendar

Call booking opens Google Calendar in a new page (external service). Google applies its own privacy policy and may place cookies on its domain.

6. How consent works

  1. When you first visit the website, a cookie banner appears with two options:
    • Accept: Loads Google Analytics.
    • Essential only: Does not load Google Analytics.
  2. Your choice is stored in localStorage (redstar-consent-v1).
  3. If you choose Accept, Google Analytics loads and places cookies.

7. How to change or withdraw your choice

You can change your choice at any time using one of these methods:

7.1 Delete website data in browser

  • Chrome/Edge: Settings → Privacy and security → Clear browsing data → Select "Cookies and other site data" → Clear.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and site data → Manage data → Remove.
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage website data → Remove.

After deletion, the cookie banner will appear again on the next visit.

7.2 Manage cookies in browser

You can configure your browser to reject all cookies or notify you when one is placed. Note: This may affect the functionality of other websites.

8. Third-party cookies

We do not use third-party cookies for ads or cross-site tracking. The only third-party cookies come from Google Analytics (consent required) and Google Fonts (functional, no tracking).
