## Design Context

### Users
Greek small and medium business owners and operators evaluating a website, e-shop, or custom business tool. They need to understand the offer quickly, compare a realistic starting price, and move confidently into a quote request or short introductory call.

### Brand Personality
Precise, confident, and warm. RedStar should feel technically capable and direct without becoming cold, futuristic, or visually aggressive. The emotional goal is informed trust.

### Aesthetic Direction
A refined editorial marketing experience built around warm off-white surfaces, crimson accents, wine-tinted neutrals, strong typographic hierarchy, and restrained motion. The existing cosmic red hero remains a distinctive brand asset; surrounding interface chrome should be quieter and more exact. Avoid generic equal-weight card grids, inflated pill shapes, decorative glows, circular price stickers, and heavy shadows.

### Design Principles
- Make the offer and next action understandable in one scan.
- Use asymmetry and hierarchy to establish a clear lead instead of treating every option equally.
- Keep brand red concentrated in actions and meaningful emphasis; let tinted neutrals carry the layout.
- Prefer hairline borders, deliberate spacing, and precise typography over decorative effects.
- Preserve keyboard access, reduced-motion support, readable Greek copy, and stable responsive behavior.
